I neglected to do a post not only the end-of-day yesterday, but also the morning today. 

The reason for the first was easy. I was in crazy-crunch mode late into the night. The reason for the second was, that I kinda soft quit yesterday. 
It wasn't a fun day. I finished up some missing art/UI and did the audio, just as planned. Then I got to this part:

> - Then back to coding and redo ingredient and base selection as a side panel
> - Add customerdb to the sidepanel

It's one thing that - possibly due to being a bit tired - I made a few bad judgments, that led me to waste a few hours on ideas that didn't work. That sucks, but I can recover from that.

But after reassessing, and looking at what's possible and what needs to be done to make it to the deadline, I didn't like the game I was making anymore. I needed to cut out all the parts that made it fun (assuming it would be fun, and the whole thing wasn't a bad idea). 

Everyone everywhere always says that the whole point of the jam is to have fun, and while I take that advice liberally - my week was a lot more Dark Souls kind of fun - trying to make a game that is bad, but functional wasn't even that anymore.

So I quit. Or rather soft-quit, because after an little rest, I went straight back to working, and I spent the whole day today working on it as well, but no longer with the goal of submitting it for the jam. 

I probably will need at least one more week to make this into a functioning prototype. The base gameplay-loop is mostly there now, though I do want to incorporate the shop into it (for now it's just opened form the devtool). And I still want to have the NPC like/dislike discovery. After that, it's just a lot of writing, because this turned out to be a story game above all else, and I need to balance the numbers. Okay, so maybe two weeks. (Also will need to sort out the Ingredient icons, because they are just randomly assigned right now).

This means I will stop posting daily jam updates, but I kind of still want to document the process, so I'll probably write a post when there is something to commemorate.

Actually no. I want to have a wrap-up post, because things were learned this jam, and I need to remember them for the next time. But sleep first.
