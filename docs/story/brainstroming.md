I am making a game. It is a story game in which a vending machine serves people, and gets to know those people through the conversations they have while they are at the machine. The people can arrive alone, or in small groups (say 2-3 coworkers at lunch). The stories will unfold at subsequent visits. There should also be some interconnectedness, as a person might not always use the vending machine with the same group of people, and in some cases we should have the ability to see a story unfold from multiple perspectives (for example both parties in a relationship use the vending machine at different times). 

The setting for this is purposefully kept a little vague, but it takes place towards the outer part of a large city, sometimes in the late 90s/early 2000s (so the internet and cell phones are commonplace, but we are definitely in the past) Give me ideas for people and their stories.






## 1. The Aspiring Musician and the Skeptical Sibling
(Note: good mid-length story, with characters we can root for. Maya needs to be tied into something else)

**Characters:**
- Leo (21): An optimistic, slightly scruffy musician. Always talking about his band's next gig, a new song he wrote, or the "big break" that's just around the corner. Often buys cheap chips.
- Maya (24): Leo's older sister. Works in a corporate office nearby. She's pragmatic and worried about Leo's future. She loves him but wishes he'd get a "real job". Buys diet soda and a granola bar.

**Story Arc:**
- Maya and Leo visit together. She's giving him money for lunch and nagging him about finding a stable job. He's trying to convince her to come to his next show.
- Leo visits alone, humming a new tune, excited because a "real producer" is coming to their next gig.
- Maya visits with a coworker, venting her worries about Leo. She's afraid he'll be disappointed again.
- Leo visits after the gig, looking dejected. The producer didn't show up. He buys the same diet soda his sister always gets.
- Maya visits alone, on her flip phone, leaving a supportive voicemail for Leo, telling him not to give up and that she's proud of him.

**Player Interactivity**
- Cheer Up Leo, Change Maya's Mind, Get Leo the Break

Things the engine currently does not support/support well.
- Telling story through phone conversations, especially text messages
- People not moving as a fixed group, some arrives later.
- Animation + Conversation *chain* at each encounter step.
- Multiple orders per person.
- Likes/Dislikes have a story (optionally). Why does the person likes/dislikes this effect? Would allow for better opportunities for serving likes/dislikes to influence the story
- Manual like/dislike discovery: NPCs can leave hints on what they like/dislike about their intended order already, so players can make notes about them, without actually generating a lile/dislike response
- Fixtures: permanent residents of a cycle (day), who may or may not order, or chat with the coming and going NPCs: a street vendor, a musician, a homeless guy camping out, a broken down bus' driver 
- Timing the fullfillment of the order. Maybe someone is in a hurry
- Special sound effects for a dialogue (a person humming to themselves)
- needs way of letting emotions know. maybe emoticons above head? (it seems jarring to end every dialogue with a description of their mood to let players know if something was just a joke, or if they asked something nervously)
- maybe narration logs? both for displaying emotions, but also for letting minor details know. like when the wrong product is served, if they take the product anyway
- insert money in multiple coins, show correct amount or insufficient amount
